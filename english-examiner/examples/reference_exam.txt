SAMPLE - fictional teacher material used to demonstrate and test My English Examiner

Gymnasium - Englisch - Klausur Q11 (1. Halbjahr)
Arbeitszeit: 90 Minuten
Hilfsmittel: einsprachiges Wörterbuch

Text: Swipe Left on Democracy? (adapted)

When politicians complain that young people do not care about politics, they usually mean that young people do not care about politicians. Turnout among first-time voters has risen in several recent elections, yet trust in parties keeps falling. Many teenagers now meet political ideas first on their phones, squeezed between dance videos and football clips. Critics warn that this turns serious debate into entertainment. Supporters reply that a thirty-second clip is still more than most adults read in a newspaper.

Tasks

1. Summarise the author's view on social media and political participation as presented in the text. (about 120 words) (10 BE)

2. Examine the means the author uses to convey their attitude towards young voters and their critics. (15 BE)

3. Choose one of the following tasks. (15 BE)
   a) "Social media makes politics more democratic." Comment on this statement. (about 250 words)
   b) Mediation: Your German exchange partner asks you how young people in Germany use social media to inform themselves about politics. Write an e-mail in English based on the German article provided. (about 200 words)

Bewertung: Inhalt und Sprache werden getrennt bewertet (siehe Bewertungsbogen).
